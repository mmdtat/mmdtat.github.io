Why Join Us

We invite culture enthusiasts and artists to become valued members of our association in Tirol.

01 / Promote Your Art

Amplify Your Voice

As a member, you gain exclusive access to our extensive social network and mailing list. Share your cultural events, exhibitions, or artistic endeavors with a community that appreciates and supports diverse expressions in Tirol.

02 / Special Privileges

20% Discount on Events

Enjoy a 20% discount on tickets for all our captivating events, including annual dance festivals, workshops, exhibitions, and performances. Connect with the heart of contemporary culture with member privileges.

03 / Cultural Impact

Support the Creative Sector

Supporting an independent artistic association creates opportunities within the creative sector, stimulates local cultural initiatives, and fosters international artistic exchange across Tirol.

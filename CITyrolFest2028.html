Festival 2027 · August 20th to 27th, 2027

What does it mean to be grounded — as artists, as humans, as communities?

01 / Physical & Philosophical Practice

Contact Improvisation as both a physical and philosophical practice — balancing between stability and flight, weight and suspension, presence and risk.

02 / Spaces for Dialogue

Beyond physical practice, the festival opens spaces for dialogue, conversations around identity, body politics, and somatic awareness.

Location & Logistics

Künstlerhaus Büchsenhausen

📍 Weiherburggasse 13, 6020 Innsbruck, Austria

Located on the slope of the Nordkette mountains just above the old town of Innsbruck, Künstlerhaus Büchsenhausen offers light-flooded studios, wooden sprung floors, and historic Alpine architecture.
